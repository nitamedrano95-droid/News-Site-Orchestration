# Gemini Analysis: NexoPress Design Specifications

## Component Dimensions (1440px layout)
- **Header**: 1440 × 80px (fijo) o 120px (extendido)
- **ArticleCard/Vertical**: 400 × 520px; Imagen (16:9): 400 × 225px
- **ArticleCard/Horizontal**: 840 × 240px; Imagen (1:1): 240 × 240px
- **TickerItem**: 48px alto; ancho variable
- **WeatherWidget**: 280 × 120px

## Typography Hierarchy (Desktop)
| Nivel | Tamaño | Fuente | Peso | Interlineado |
|-------|--------|--------|------|--------------|
| Hero Title | 56px | Playfair Display | Bold (700) | 1.1 |
| Article Title | 32px | Playfair Display | SemiBold (600) | 1.2 |
| Subtitle | 20px | Source Sans Pro | Regular (400) | 1.5 |
| Body text | 18px | Source Sans Pro | Regular (400) | 1.6 |
| Meta/Tags | 14px | Source Sans Pro | Bold (700) | Uppercase |

## Category Color Palette
- **Economía**: `#1a1a2e` (principal dark) o `#00d4aa` (acento)
- **Política**: `#00d4aa` (color primario)
- **Tecnología**: `#4a90e2` (azul tech)
- **Deportes**: `#ff6b6b` (rojo coral)

## Design Principles
- Use teal (`#00d4aa`) as universal hover/active state across all sections
- Maintain visual hierarchy through typography weight, not color saturation
- Left-align content; use 80px margins at 1440px canvas
- Category badges should be prominent and differentiated by color
